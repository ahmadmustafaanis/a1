# Lumen: hackathon film storylines (≤ 80 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

## A. Grey to understood (picked)
Device: life before and after → the result first. Form: object-to-object chain, the grey CT turns into the body.
The viewer sees a real CT scroll by as grey noise they can't read. Then the same scan becomes their own anatomy in 3D, and we follow one real task: Opus builds the scene, you pull it apart, Opus catches an 8 mm spot that the small model missed, rewrites that model's weights live, shows why, and writes the report.
First 2 s: full-frame axial CT slices flicking past like a radiologist scrolling, with "This is a CT scan." landing in white.
Beats: hook (CT scroll) → tension (grey, jargon) → reveal (colour flood, "Lumen") → proof (build · pull apart · the finding) → payoff (weights rewritten, 7 in 10 → 9 in 10) → why (X-ray) → report → end card.
Look: two worlds, the CT world (real grey CT on black, the medium itself) and Lumen's own bright product ground (#F4F3EF, ink #111214, cobalt #2F5BFF, finding red #FF3B30, Geist).
Sound: a minimal pulse that stays thin under the CT, opens when the colour floods in, lifts at the payoff; clicks only on real cursor clicks, one whoosh per world change.

## B. Opus at work
Device: a character does the work (Opus's own process feed as the narrator). Form: one continuous take inside the app.
The film never leaves the product: Opus's thinking lines become the titles, and each tool it calls cuts to what it built.

## C. 0.29
Device: a number lands. Form: split screen to one.
Opens on "0.29", the model's score for a real 8 mm spot, and the whole film earns the moment it becomes 0.64.
